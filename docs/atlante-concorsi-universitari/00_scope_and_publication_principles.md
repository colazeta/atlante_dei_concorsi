# 00 — Scope and publication principles

## Stage objective
This module defines the methodological backbone and minimum data structures for a documentary atlas of Italian university recruitment procedures.

At this stage, the atlas records only:
1. documentary facts;
2. derived descriptive indicators;
3. potential signals that require human verification.

Legal or disciplinary assessment is outside scope.

## Core separation principle
Each stored record must be attributable to exactly one level:
- **Documentary fact**: directly stated in a public document.
- **Derived descriptive indicator**: calculated from coded documentary fields.
- **Potential review signal**: a non-conclusive cue to prioritize human review.
- **Legal/disciplinary assessment**: never produced by this atlas.

## Editorial language policy
Use neutral and non-accusatory wording, including:
- documented relationship;
- profile with high descriptive specificity;
- criteria with high degree of articulation;
- potentially relevant relation for human review;
- not determinable from available public documentation.

Avoid labels such as “rigged”, “corrupt”, “suspicious”, or “conflict confirmed”.

## Publication policy
- Publish verifiable facts and clearly explain indicator construction.
- Mark uncertain fields as `not_determinable` when appropriate.
- Keep a transparent trace of source URLs, retrieval dates, and versions.
- Require human review before publishing relation-sensitive summaries.
